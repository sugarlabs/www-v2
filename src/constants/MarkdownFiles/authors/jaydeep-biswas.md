# About Jaydeep Biswas

Hi, I'm Jaydeep, an open-source contributor who enjoys exploring new ideas, experimenting with technology, and learning how things work.

Music has always been something I've enjoyed, even though I don't have a deep understanding of music theory or musical notes. Growing up, I tried playing the piano and harmonium, and I also spent some time learning guitar. While I never pursued music seriously, my curiosity and interest in it have stayed with me.

When I discovered Music Blocks, I became fascinated by how programming and music could come together in such a creative and interactive way. It gave me an opportunity to explore two things I enjoy: understanding how technology works and experimenting with music.

Through contributing to Music Blocks, I've been learning not only about programming, debugging, and problem-solving, but also about how creativity and technology can make learning more engaging and accessible for children/youths.

I'm still learning, experimenting, making mistakes, and discovering new things along the way, and that's what makes this journey exciting for me.
